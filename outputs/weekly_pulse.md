# Weekly Product Pulse: ChatGPT Play Store (India), week ending 2026-11-15
Based on 2377 reviews over 12 weeks.

## Summary of top themes
1. **Accuracy**: 463 of 566 reviews were 1-2 star (82%); last 4 weeks 189 vs 190 before. Accuracy issues rise as answers become more incorrect and contradictory.
2. **Limits & Paywall**: 411 of 531 reviews were 1-2 star (77%); last 4 weeks 160 vs 199 before. Limits and paywall complaints grow as free usage shrinks.
3. **Performance**: 361 of 450 reviews were 1-2 star (80%); last 4 weeks 135 vs 173 before. Performance degrades with slower responses, crashes, and glitchy scrolling.

## Supporting user quotes
1. "awful, gives wrong answers much more often than right answers, contradicts itself all the time." (1 star, Accuracy)
2. "They keep making the free version dumber with every update, and now hit limit 5x faster,it's terrible and completely useless." (1 star, Limits & Paywall)
3. "ChatGPT is even worse than before, the responds take one day or it never happens, the response button is still laggy." (2 star, Performance)

## Key observation
These problems together worsen user trust, especially when billing confusion adds frustration.
Fee confusion: 21 of 172 money-related reviews, mostly about billing issues.

## 3 action ideas
1. Improve answer verification to reduce hallucinations and contradictions. (Quote 1)
2. Rebalance free limits and clarify upgrade benefits to ease frustration. (Quote 2)
3. Optimize app stability and transparently explain billing to prevent confusion. (Quote 3)
