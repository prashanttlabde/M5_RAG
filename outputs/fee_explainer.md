# Fee explainer: billing issues

- Credit card payments can be declined due to bank, card, authentication, or regional issues. [openai_3943089]
- Updating your API payment method may create a temporary $5 authorization hold. [openai_3943089]
- For an unused individual subscription purchase, a discretionary refund may be available if you contact us within seven days of the charge. [openai_7232895]
- Brazil‑registered accounts using a Brazil‑issued payment card are eligible for a full refund if requested within 7 days of the charge. [openai_7232895]
- There is no automatic prorated discretionary refund after seven days of the charge. [openai_7232895]
- Annual billing or paying for multiple months in advance is not supported for ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscriptions. [openai_11989085]
