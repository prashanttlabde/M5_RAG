# Fee explainer: billing issues

- Credit card payments can be declined due to bank, card, authentication, or regional issues. [openai_3943089]
- Updating your API payment method may create a temporary $5 authorization hold. [openai_3943089]
- Payment failures can occur because of issues with automatic recharges, balance limits, or expired credits. [openai_3943089]
- You can cancel a ChatGPT subscription purchased on the web, Apple App Store, or Google Play through the appropriate cancellation process. [openai_3943089]
- OpenAI does not support annual billing or advance payment for ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscriptions. [openai_11989085]
- When a credit card is declined, checking your bank, card details, authentication, and region can help resolve the issue. [openai_3943089]
