You can find past invoices for ChatGPT subscriptions purchased on the web in your billing settings. The steps differ for Go, Plus, or Pro subscriptions and Business workspaces.
For subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, use the store receipt instructions below.
Go, Plus, or Pro
- In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings > Billing and find Invoices.
- In the Invoices section, select View beside an invoice to open it. Select View all to see more invoices.
Troubleshooting
- If your Invoices section is missing or empty, make sure you’re signed into the correct ChatGPT account or workspace. Receipts for subscriptions started through the Apple App Store or Google Play won’t appear here—check your app-store purchase history instead.
- If the billing controls needed to view your invoices are missing, sign in to the same ChatGPT account in a different browser, then try again. If you bought your subscription on the web and the invoice is still missing, contact Support with the billing period and the invoice number, if you have it.
- If an invoice you found needs a correction, see how to request a review of an issued invoice.
Business
From your workspace settings, select Billing, then Invoices to view invoices available for that workspace/account. If none appear, confirm you are an owner on the correct workspace/account and that invoice records exist. If you still can’t locate invoices, contact OpenAI Support with your account email.
You can also select Manage plan > Payment method to open your customer billing portal and view past invoices.
Find invoices for a deactivated workspace
If you are a workspace owner and cannot open Billing, contact Support for help locating past invoices. Include your account email, workspace name or ID if known, the billing periods you need, and any previous invoice number. Tell us whether the workspace is deactivated and which billing page you cannot access.
Apple App Store receipts
Use the Apple Account that paid for your subscription. Its email address may differ from your ChatGPT email. Apple sends an email receipt to that account each time your ChatGPT subscription renews.
Use your iPhone or iPad
- Open the App Store and select your photo or initials. If prompted, select My Account and sign in.
- Select Apps & Purchase History.
- Select Purchase History.
- Find your ChatGPT purchase. Change the date filter if you need to find an older charge.
Use a computer or browser
Open Apple’s purchase history, sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, and find the ChatGPT charge. If it is missing, check any other Apple Account you may have used. See Apple’s purchase history instructions for other device options.
Google Play receipts
Use the Google account that paid for your subscription. Google sends purchase confirmations to that account’s email address.
- Open the Google Play Store app.
- Select your profile icon.
- Select Payments & subscriptions.
- Select Budget & history and find your ChatGPT order.
You can also check purchases on the web. See Google Play’s order history instructions.
