Developers located in Australia
If you're located in Australia, you're responsible for determining, charging and remitting GST on all Google Play Store paid-app and in-app purchases made by customers in Australia.
If you are not GST registered, Google may charge 10% GST on the service fee payable from you to Google. The 10% GST charge will be reflected separately from the service fee, where applicable.
Your tax registration status determines the taxes that you're charged in Australia. Here's how you can provide Google with your Australia tax information:
- Sign in to Play Console and go to the Payments profile page (Manage account > Play apps settings > Monetisation settings > Payments profile).
- In the 'Settings' section, click Manage settings.
- Scroll to 'Australia tax info' and click the Edit icon .
- Select the declaration tick box, and enter your Australia Business Number (ABN) in the 'Australia Business Number' field.
- Click Save.
Pursuant to the Sharing Economy Reporting Regime in Australia, Google is required to collect certain information from you and you should update your profile with the following information (if not already provided to Google):
- Date of birth (for individual developers);
- ABN (for non-individual developers); and
- Contact numbers.
